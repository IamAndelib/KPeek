1.0.6
- Markdown files with HTML images, such as the centred logos and screenshots of many READMEs, now show in full.

1.0.5
- Previews files in the Trash.
- Pressing the shortcut with nothing selected no longer shows an error.

1.0.4: security and robustness
- A Markdown file can no longer freeze KPeek or read devices through its images.
- KPeek never runs Ghostscript, even on PostScript disguised as another file type.
- 3D models count the files they reference before loading on their own.
- Files under /usr and /etc show the system's own file, not the sandbox's.
- The installer checks the download against the release's checksum.
- Starts a little faster.

1.0.3
- Resizing from the top edge and top corners now works on Wayland too.

1.0.2
- The window can be resized from its top edge and top corners too.

1.0.1
- KPeek quits when you close a preview: nothing stays running in the background.
- A borderless preview window: drag the header bar to move it, drag its edges to resize it.

1.0.0: first stable release.
- Previews images, PDFs, comic books, office documents, video and audio, text and code, Markdown, 3D models, archives and fonts.
- Arrow keys move through the selection or the whole folder; zoom with Ctrl and the mouse wheel or the header buttons; drag to pan.
- Works with Dolphin on any desktop and distribution with Flatpak; opens files read-only, never accesses the network, runs sandboxed.
