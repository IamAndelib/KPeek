1.0.1
- KPeek quits when you close a preview: nothing stays running in the background.

1.0.0: first stable release.
- Previews images, PDFs, comic books, office documents, video and audio, text and code, Markdown, 3D models, archives and fonts.
- Arrow keys move through the selection or the whole folder; zoom with Ctrl and the mouse wheel or the header buttons; drag to pan.
- Works with Dolphin on any desktop and distribution with Flatpak; opens files read-only, never accesses the network, runs sandboxed.
